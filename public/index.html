Learn from the seniors who have been where you are.

Campora is the Learning Bridge between junior students and seniors. Ask a question, get matched with a Helper who knows the topic, chat, and grow your campus network. Helpers earn points they can redeem for campus perks.

For students looking for help

Seeker

Post a question, search for seniors by skill or department, and get guidance on courses, projects and careers.

Continue as a Seeker
Skill matching connects the two sides
For senior students who guide others

Helper

Answer questions in your area, share campus events, climb the leaderboard and redeem points for benefits.

Continue as a Helper

From a stuck moment to a solved problem

Step 1

Ask or search

Post a public question or browse Helpers by skill, department, course and year.

Step 2

Get matched

A rule-based skill matcher compares your topic with Helper skills and explains why each one fits.

Step 3

Chat

Once a Helper accepts, a private one-to-one chat opens for the two of you.

Step 4

Earn and redeem

Completed help earns points. Spend them on demo campus benefits with a QR code.

Built for both sides of the bridge

Seekers can

  • Search Helpers and open full profiles
  • Send a request to one Helper or post publicly
  • Track requests from pending to completed
  • Follow hackathons, workshops and campus events

Helpers can

  • Accept, reject and complete requests
  • Publish campus announcements
  • Earn points and appear on the leaderboard
  • Redeem points for demo campus benefits
